Smudge is installed

Blur anything on a page before you screen share or record. Here's how it works.

1

Pin it, then click the icon

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Smudge so it's always visible.

2

Turn Blur Mode On

Click the Smudge icon, then click Turn Blur Mode On. Your cursor becomes a crosshair — that means it's active.

3

Click anything to blur it

Hover over an element on the page — it outlines in red. Click it once to blur it, click again to unblur.

4

Or drag to draw a box

Click and drag anywhere to draw a box over any area — mid-sentence text, part of an image, anything that isn't a single clean element. Click a drawn box again to remove it.

5

It's saved automatically

Reload the page and your blurred elements and boxes are still there. Use Clear All Blurs Here in the popup to reset a page.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
